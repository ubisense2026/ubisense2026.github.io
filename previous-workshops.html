UbiSense 2025

Date October 13, 2025
Location Aalto University, Espoo, Finland (co-located with UbiComp/ISWC 2025)
Theme AI-driven ubiquitous sensing with LLMs and foundation models

The inaugural UbiSense 2025 workshop was held at UbiComp/ISWC 2025 in Espoo, Finland, attracting over 75 participants and ranking as one of the largest workshops at the conference. The workshop featured keynote talks by Thomas Ploetz (Georgia Institute of Technology), Chiara Boldrini (IIT-CNR), and Stephan Sigg (Aalto University), along with 16 accepted paper presentations covering topics such as LLMs for sensor data, multimodal sensing, and privacy-aware computing.

For more information, please visit the UbiSense 2025 website.
